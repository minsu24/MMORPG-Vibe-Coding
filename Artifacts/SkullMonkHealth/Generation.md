Transparent outside AND inside opening, genuine alpha. Keep artwork compact and tightly framed. Absolutely NO upper plaque, NO nameplate, NO title region, NO space reserved for a boss name, NO upper crest, NO floating ornaments, NO characters, NO letters including HP, NO numbers, NO text, NO background, NO glow cloud, NO painted health fill, NO gradients inside the opening, NO multiple bars or variants.
